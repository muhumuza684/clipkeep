How it works
  1. Copy anything in any app. It is saved here on its own.
  2. Press Copy on an item, then press Ctrl+V where you want it.
  3. Or press Paste and ClipKeep types it into the app you were just using.

      Open with Win+V

      Windows does not have a "default clipboard" setting that apps can ask for. Its own clipboard history owns the Win+V shortcut. ClipKeep can take Win+V over by switching Windows' own Win+V shortcut off for your account. You can undo it here at any time.

      Your taskbar will flash and open File Explorer windows will close. Or sign out and back in.
      There you can turn off Windows' own clipboard history if you want only ClipKeep.
